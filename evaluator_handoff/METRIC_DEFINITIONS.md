# Metric definitions

## Primary

**Grounded correct answer rate** — fraction of *all* questions scored correct under the registered adjudication route (automated or completed human). Denominator = all questions.

## Secondary

- Path validity / provenance coverage (graph systems)
- Abstention appropriateness
- Latency p50/p95/p99 and peak RSS
- Retrieval recall@k / MRR (retrieval arms only; not comparable to answer correctness)

## Families

Do not mix **controlled** and **system-level** scores in one headline statistic.
